About DUA SCHOOL

DUA SCHOOL is dedicated to providing high-quality education in a safe, supportive, and inspiring learning environment. We believe that every student has unique talents and the potential to succeed. Our experienced teachers encourage students to develop academic excellence, creativity, confidence, and strong moral values.

At DUA SCHOOL, we combine modern teaching methods with character development to prepare students for future challenges. Our goal is to help every learner become a responsible citizen, a lifelong learner, and a confident leader who can make a positive difference in society.

Our courses

Primary Education

Our Primary Education program provides a strong foundation in English, Mathematics, Science, Urdu, Islamiat, and General Knowledge. We focus on developing reading, writing, communication, creativity, and problem-solving skills in a caring learning environment.

Middle Education

Our Middle Education program prepares students for higher studies by strengthening their knowledge in Mathematics, Science, English, Computer Studies, Social Studies, and Islamiat. Students are encouraged to think critically, work collaboratively, and build confidence.
